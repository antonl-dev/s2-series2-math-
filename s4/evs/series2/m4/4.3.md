# 4.3 Waste Disposal Methods (Landfill, Incineration, Recycling)

## 1. Landfill
Landfill is the oldest form of waste treatment, involving the disposal of waste materials by burial.

### Modern Sanitary Landfills
Unlike open dumping, modern sanitary landfills are carefully engineered structures designed to isolate waste from the environment.
*   **Bottom Liner:** Sealed with layers of clay and coated with high-density plastic to prevent groundwater contamination.
*   **Leachate Collection System:** Pipes installed to collect the highly toxic liquid (leachate) that accumulates at the bottom.
*   **Methane Collection System:** Traps inflammable gases (like methane) produced by decomposition, which can be used to generate energy.
*   **Cover:** Daily layers of soil are placed over waste to prevent odors, pests, and fire hazards.

### Methods of Landfilling:
1.  **Area Method:** Used on flat or gently sloping land with natural depressions.
2.  **Trench Method:** Ideal for small quantities of waste; involves excavating a trench, filling it with waste, and covering it with the excavated soil.
3.  **Ramp/Slope Method:** Waste is spread and compacted along the side of an existing hill slope.
4.  **Valley Method:** Waste is deposited in natural canyons or ravines.
5.  **Cell Method:** A progressive concept where waste is deposited in pre-constructed bonded areas called cells.

## 2. Incineration
Incineration is a thermal treatment process (combustion) where solid waste is burnt at high temperatures ($800^{\circ}C$ to $1000^{\circ}C$).

*   **Process:** Waste is converted into heat, gas, steam, and ash.
*   **Volume Reduction:** It reduces the original waste volume by **20-30%**.
*   **Energy Recovery:** Steam generated from the heat is used to drive turbines and produce electricity.
*   **Air Pollution Control:** Modern incinerators use specialized filters to trap toxic pollutants and fly ash before smoke is released.

### Types of Incinerators:
*   Fluidized bed
*   Multiple hearth incinerator
*   Injection incinerator

### Pyrolysis (Alternative Thermal Treatment):
Heating organic substances in a **closed container without oxygen**. It splits the waste into gaseous, liquid, and solid fractions (char). This process is highly endothermic.

## 3. Recycling
Recycling is the process of salvaging valuable materials from the waste stream so they can be used again.

*   **Primary Recycling:** The original waste material is processed back into the **same material** (e.g., used office paper into new paper).
*   **Secondary Recycling:** Waste materials are converted into **different products** (e.g., used tires shredded for road pavements, or food waste into compost).

### Benefits of Recycling:
*   Conserves natural resources.
*   Reduces the need for energy-intensive extraction of raw materials.
*   Decreases the load on landfills and incinerators.

---

## 4. Comparison Summary

| Method | Advantages | Disadvantages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Landfill** | Simple, economical, can recover low-lying land. | Evolution of landfill gases, toxic leachate risk, requires large area. |
| **Incineration** | Efficient volume reduction, generates heat/power, eliminates germs. | Expensive to build/operate, potential air pollution, produces toxic ash. |
| **Recycling** | Environmentally friendly, conserves resources, promotes sustainability. | Limited potential in emergencies, requires segregation at source, expensive setup. |
