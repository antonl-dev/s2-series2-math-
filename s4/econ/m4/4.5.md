# Module 4: Section 4.5 - Methods of Measuring National Income and Problems

### 1. Methods of Measuring National Income
There are three main ways to calculate National Income, all of which should theoretically yield the same result.

#### A) Product Method (or Value Added Method)
This method calculates the total value of all final goods and services produced within the domestic territory.
*   **Formula:** $GVA_{mp} = \text{Gross Value of Output} - \text{Intermediate Consumption}$.
*   **Step:** Sum of $GVA_{mp}$ for all sectors (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary) gives $GDP_{mp}$.
*   **Note:** We subtract Intermediate Consumption to avoid the problem of **Double Counting** (counting the value of a product more than once).

#### B) Final Expenditure Method
This method totals the spending by all sectors of the economy.
*   **Formula:** $GDP_{mp} = C + I + G + (X - M)$
    *   **C:** Private Final Consumption Expenditure
    *   **I:** Investment Expenditure
    *   **G:** Government Consumption Expenditure
    *   **X - M:** Net Exports (Exports - Imports)

#### C) Income Method
This method totals the income earned by the factors of production.
*   **Formula:** $NDP_{fc} = \text{Rent} + \text{Interest} + \text{Wages} + \text{Profit} + \text{Mixed Income of Self-employed}$.
*   **National Income ($NNP_{fc}$):** $NDP_{fc} + NFIA$.

---

### 2. Basic National Income Equations
*   **$NDP_{mp} = GDP_{mp} - \text{Depreciation}$**
*   **$NNP_{mp} = NDP_{mp} + NFIA$** (Net Factor Income from Abroad)
*   **$NNP_{fc} = NNP_{mp} - NIT$** (Net Indirect Tax)
*   **$NIT = \text{Indirect Tax} - \text{Subsidy}$**
*   **$NNP_{fc}$** is the standard definition of **National Income**.

---

### 3. Items Excluded from National Income
Certain transactions do not represent new production and are excluded:
1.  **Transfer Payments:** (Pensions, scholarships, gifts).
2.  **Sale of Second-hand Goods:** (Value was already counted when produced).
3.  **Illegal Activities:** (Smuggling, gambling).
4.  **Financial Transactions:** Buying and selling of shares/bonds (only transfer of ownership).
5.  **Intermediate Goods:** To avoid double counting.

---

### 4. Important Calculations (Aggregates)
*   **Per Capita Income:** $\frac{\text{National Income}}{\text{Total Population}}$
*   **Personal Disposable Income:** $\text{Personal Income} - \text{Direct Taxes}$ (Income left for spending/saving).
