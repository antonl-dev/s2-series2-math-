# Module 4: Section 4.2 - Post-Reform Growth, Structure of Productive Activity, and Issues of Inclusion

### 1. The 1991 Economic Reforms (LPG)
In 1991, India faced a severe Balance of Payments (BoP) crisis. To recover, the government introduced the **New Economic Policy**, focusing on three pillars:
*   **L - Liberalization:** Ending the "License Raj" and reducing government control over the private sector.
*   **P - Privatization:** Reducing the role of the public sector by selling government stakes in PSUs (Disinvestment).
*   **G - Globalization:** Integrating the Indian economy with the global market by reducing trade barriers and inviting foreign investment.

### 2. Post-Reform Growth & Structure of Activity
After 1991, the structure of the Indian economy shifted significantly away from the traditional model:
*   **Service Sector Dominance:** India bypassed the traditional transition from agriculture to manufacturing. Instead, the **Services Sector** (IT, Banking, Telecom) became the largest contributor to GDP (over 50%).
*   **Decline in Agriculture:** While agriculture still employs nearly half of the workforce, its share in the GDP has fallen drastically.
*   **Stagnant Manufacturing:** Despite initiatives like "Make in India," the manufacturing sector's growth has been slower compared to services.

### 3. Issues of Inclusion (Inclusive Growth)
"Inclusive Growth" means economic growth that creates employment opportunities and helps in reducing poverty across all sections of society.
*   **The Problem:** While GDP grew rapidly after 1991, the benefits were not distributed equally, leading to **Income Inequality**.
*   **Key Issues:**
    *   **Jobless Growth:** GDP increased, but employment did not grow at the same rate.
    *   **Rural-Urban Divide:** Urban areas and service-oriented cities grew much faster than rural agricultural areas.
    *   **Gender Gap:** Lower participation of women in the formal workforce.
    *   **Access to Basic Services:** Unequal access to quality healthcare, education, and digital infrastructure in remote areas.

### 4. Government Measures for Inclusion
*   **MGNREGA:** Guaranteed 100 days of wage employment to rural households.
*   **Digital India & Jan Dhan Yojana:** To ensure financial inclusion (banking for all).
*   **Skill India:** To make the youth employable in the modern economy.
