# Module 1.2: Scarcity, Choice, PPC, and the Firm

### 1.2.1 The Problem of Scarcity and Choice
According to your notes, the "Science of Choice" is the foundation of economics.

*   **The Conflict:** Human needs/wants are **unlimited**, but the resources to satisfy them are **scarce** (limited).
*   **Alternative Uses:** Resources can be used for more than one purpose (e.g., land can be used for a factory or a hospital). Therefore, we must make a **Choice**.
*   **The Central Problems (Exam Favorite - 2 Marks):**
    1.  **What to produce?** Choosing goods based on demand and technology (e.g., Hospitals vs. Defense).
    2.  **How to produce?** Choosing the cost-effective technique.
        *   *Labor Intensive:* Using more workers.
        *   *Capital Intensive:* Using more machines/technology.
    3.  **For whom to produce?** How to distribute the goods among different sections of society.

### 1.2.2 Production Possibility Curve (PPC)
**Definition:** The PPC is a curve (or locus) showing all possible combinations of two goods that an economy can produce using all available resources efficiently with a given level of technology.

**Key Features (From Page 22 of your notes):**
1.  **Scarcity:** Producing more of one good (e.g., Capital Goods) means producing less of another (e.g., Consumer Goods).
2.  **Attainable vs. Unattainable:**
    *   **Points ON the curve:** Full employment and efficient use of resources.
    *   **Points INSIDE the curve:** Inefficiency or under-utilization of resources.
    *   **Points OUTSIDE the curve:** Unattainable combinations with current resources/technology.

**The "Make in India" Case Study (From Question Paper):**
*   **Question:** How does a campaign like "Make in India" affect the PPC?
*   **Answer:** It shifts the PPC to the **Right**. This is because it attracts more investment (resources) and better technology, increasing the economy's total productive capacity.

### 1.2.3 The Firm and its Objectives
**Definition:** A firm is an individual production unit that transforms inputs (factors of production) into useful outputs.

**Factors of Production (Input):**
*   Raw materials
*   Labor
*   Capital/Machines
*   Organization/Management

**Objectives of a Modern Firm (Exam Topic - 4 Marks):**
In modern economics, firms don't just look at profit. Their objectives include:
1.  **Profit Maximization:** The traditional goal ($Total Revenue > Total Cost$).
2.  **Sales Maximization:** Trying to capture the highest market share.
3.  **Growth:** Expanding the size of the company over the long term.
4.  **Utility Maximization:** (Williamson’s Model) Managers may seek to maximize their own satisfaction through staff expenditure or perks.
5.  **Social Responsibility:** Providing value to consumers and the community (satisfying consumers).

### 1.2.4 Opportunity Cost (OC)
**Definition:** Opportunity cost is the value of the **next best alternative foregone** when a choice is made.
*   *Example from your notes:* If a student chooses to study full-time, the opportunity cost is the salary they would have earned from a part-time job.
