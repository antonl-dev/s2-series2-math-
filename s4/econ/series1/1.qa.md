# Module 1: Question and Answer Bank

## Part A: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks)

### Q1. Define Marginal Utility (MU).
**Answer:** Marginal utility is the additional utility (satisfaction) gained from consuming one additional unit of a commodity. 
*   **Formula:** $MU = \frac{\Delta TU}{\Delta Q}$

### Q2. Define Consumer Surplus.
**Answer:** Consumer Surplus is the difference between the maximum price a consumer is willing to pay for a product (based on utility) and the actual price they pay in the market.
*   **Formula:** $Consumer Surplus = \text{Willingness to Pay} - \text{Actual Price}$.

### Q3. What are the central problems of an economy? Why do they occur?
**Answer:** The three central problems are:
1.  **What to produce?** (Selection of goods).
2.  **How to produce?** (Selection of technique: Labor vs. Capital intensive).
3.  **For whom to produce?** (Distribution of goods).
*   **Reason:** These occur because human wants are **unlimited**, but resources are **scarce** and have **alternative uses**.

### Q4. "Make in India" campaign would shift the PPC to the right. How?
**Answer:** The "Make in India" campaign aims to increase investment, improve technology, and enhance infrastructure. This increases the total productive capacity of the country. Since the PPC represents the maximum possible output, an increase in resources/technology shifts the curve outward to the right.

### Q5. State the Law of Demand.
**Answer:** The Law of Demand states that, other things being equal (Ceteris Paribus), there is an inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded. When the price of a good falls, its demand rises, and when the price rises, its demand falls.

### Q6. Explain the social responsibility of business.
**Answer:** Social responsibility (CSR) means that a firm should not just focus on profit, but also act for the benefit of society. This includes protecting the environment, ensuring fair wages for labor, providing quality goods to consumers, and contributing to social welfare.

### Q7. Distinguish between CRR and SLR.
**Answer:** 
*   **CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio):** The percentage of total deposits that banks must keep as cash with the Central Bank (RBI).
*   **SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio):** The percentage of total deposits that banks must maintain with themselves in liquid form (Cash, Gold, or Govt. securities).

## Part B: Short Essay Questions (4 - 6 Marks)

### Q8. List four advantages of Co-operative societies.
**Answer:**
1.  **Democratic Management:** Managed on the principle of "One member, one vote."
2.  **Service Motive:** The primary objective is to provide services/benefits to members rather than maximizing profit.
3.  **Elimination of Middlemen:** They buy directly from producers and sell to members, reducing costs (e.g., AMUL).
4.  **Limited Liability:** The liability of members is limited to the extent of their capital contribution.

### Q9. Describe the main objectives of a firm in a modern economy.
**Answer:**
1.  **Profit Maximization:** The traditional goal of earning maximum surplus ($TR > TC$).
2.  **Sales Maximization:** Increasing market share to establish brand dominance.
3.  **Growth Maximization:** Long-term expansion of assets and production capacity.
4.  **Utility Maximization:** Satisfying the goals of managers and employees (Williamson’s Model).
5.  **Social Satisfaction:** Providing value and quality to ensure consumer loyalty.

### Q10. Define Price Elasticity of Demand. What are the factors affecting it?
**Answer:** Price Elasticity ($E_p$) measures the responsiveness of quantity demanded to a change in price.
**Factors affecting $E_p$:**
1.  **Nature of commodity:** Necessities (Inelastic) vs. Luxuries (Elastic).
2.  **Availability of substitutes:** More substitutes = More Elastic.
3.  **Income level:** High-income groups are less sensitive to price (Inelastic).
4.  **Time period:** Demand is more elastic in the long run.
5.  **Proportion of income spent:** Small expenses (like salt) are inelastic.

### Q11. Numerical: Calculate Price Elasticity of Demand.
**Question:** Initial price of ice cream is ₹4 and demand is 1 cup. If the price falls to ₹2, demand increases to 4 cups. Find Elasticity using the percentage method.
**Answer:**
*   $P_1 = 4, Q_1 = 1$
*   $P_2 = 2, Q_2 = 4$
*   $\Delta P = 2, \Delta Q = 3$
*   **Formula:** $E_p = \frac{\Delta Q}{Q_1} \times \frac{P_1}{\Delta P}$
*   **Calculation:** $\frac{3}{1} \times \frac{4}{2} = 3 \times 2 = \mathbf{6}$
*   **Inference:** Demand is highly elastic ($E_p > 1$).

## Part C: Long Essay / Numerical Questions (8 - 10 Marks)

### Q12. Numerical: Market Equilibrium.
**Question:** $D = 60 - 2P$ and $S = 30 + 4P$. Find (i) Equilibrium Price and Quantity, (ii) Excess supply when Price = ₹6.
**Answer:**
**(i) Equilibrium:**
Set $D = S \rightarrow 60 - 2P = 30 + 4P$
$30 = 6P \rightarrow \mathbf{P = ₹5}$
Substitute $P$ in $D$: $60 - 2(5) = \mathbf{Q = 50 \text{ units}}$

**(ii) Price = ₹6:**
Demand = $60 - 2(6) = 48$
Supply = $30 + 4(6) = 54$
**Excess Supply** = $54 - 48 = \mathbf{6 \text{ units}}$.

### Q13. Discuss the Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility (LDMU).
**Answer:**
*   **Statement:** As a person consumes more and more units of a specific commodity, the utility derived from each successive unit goes on diminishing.
*   **Assumptions:** Homogeneous units, continuous consumption, standard size of units.
*   **Relationship:** 
    1. When MU is positive, TU increases.
    2. When MU is zero, TU is maximum (Satiety point).
    3. When MU is negative, TU decreases.
*   **Graph:** [Draw the downward sloping MU curve and the rising then falling TU curve].

### Q14. Explain the concept of Production Possibility Curve (PPC).
**Answer:**
*   **Definition:** PPC is a curve showing all possible combinations of two goods an economy can produce with fixed resources and technology.
*   **Features:**
    1.  **Downward Sloping:** Due to scarcity (to get more of A, you must give up some of B).
    2.  **Concave to Origin:** Due to increasing opportunity cost.
*   **Points:**
    *   **On the curve:** Efficient use of resources.
    *   **Inside the curve:** Inefficiency/Under-utilization.
    *   **Outside the curve:** Unattainable with current resources.
