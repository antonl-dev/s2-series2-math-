# Module 2.5: Revenue Concepts and the Shut Down Point

## 2.5.1 Revenue Concepts
Revenue is the total amount of money a firm receives from the sale of its goods. (From pages 50 & 52 of your notes).

1.  **Total Revenue (TR):** Total money received.
    *   **Formula:** $TR = Price (P) \times Quantity (Q)$
2.  **Average Revenue (AR):** Revenue per unit sold. This is the same as the **Price**.
    *   **Formula:** $AR = TR / Q$
3.  **Marginal Revenue (MR):** The additional revenue earned by selling one more unit.
    *   **Formula:** $MR = \Delta TR / \Delta Q$ (Change in Total Revenue).

### Revenue under Different Markets:
*   **Perfect Competition:** The firm is a "price taker." Price remains constant regardless of quantity sold. Here, **Price = AR = MR**.
*   **Imperfect Competition (Monopoly/Oligopoly):** To sell more, the firm must lower the price. 
    *   Result: The AR curve slopes downward.
    *   The **MR curve is always BELOW the AR curve** (because lowering price for the next unit also lowers it for previous units).

## 2.5.2 The Shut Down Point (SDP)
**Definition:** The Shut Down Point is the minimum price at which a firm can justify staying open in the **short run**. (From page 53).

### The Condition:
A firm should shut down if the **Price (AR) falls below the Average Variable Cost (AVC)**.
> **Condition:** $Price < AVC$

### Why keep producing if you are making a loss?
In the short run, a firm has to pay its **Fixed Costs** (like rent) even if it produces zero.
*   If the Revenue covers **all Variable Costs** and even a **tiny part of the Fixed Costs**, it is better to stay open.
*   If Revenue doesn't even cover the electricity and raw materials (Variable Costs), every unit produced adds *more* to the loss than shutting down.

## 2.5.3 Mathematical Example (From Page 53)
**Case Study:**
*   Variable Cost (AVC): ₹10,000
*   Fixed Cost (TFC): ₹20,000
*   **Total Cost (TC):** $10k + 20k = ₹30,000$

**Scenario A: Revenue = ₹11,000**
*   Revenue (11k) is **greater** than Variable Cost (10k).
*   Current Loss: $30k - 11k = ₹19,000$.
*   If they shut down: Loss = ₹20,000 (The full Fixed Cost).
*   **Decision:** **STAY OPEN**. (Losing 19k is better than losing 20k).

**Scenario B: Revenue = ₹9,000**
*   Revenue (9k) is **less** than Variable Cost (10k).
*   Current Loss: $30k - 9k = ₹21,000$.
*   If they shut down: Loss = ₹20,000.
*   **Decision:** **SHUT DOWN**. (Losing 21k is worse than losing 20k).
