# Module 1.7: Market Equilibrium

## 1.7.1 What is Equilibrium?
**Definition:** Market Equilibrium is a state where the quantity demanded by consumers exactly equals the quantity supplied by producers. It is the "safe point" or the point where the market clears (From page 13 & 31 of your notes).

*   **Equilibrium Price:** The price at which $Qty Demanded = Qty Supplied$.
*   **Equilibrium Quantity:** The amount bought and sold at the equilibrium price.

## 1.7.3 Market Disequilibrium
If the price is not at the equilibrium level, market forces will push it back:
1.  **Excess Demand (Shortage):** Occurs when Price < Equilibrium Price. Consumers want more than what is available. This competition among buyers pushes the price **up**.
2.  **Excess Supply (Surplus):** Occurs when Price > Equilibrium Price. Producers have more goods than buyers want. To get rid of stock, producers push the price **down**.

---

## 1.7.4 Effects of Changes (Shifts)
When the Demand or Supply curve shifts (due to factors other than price), the equilibrium point changes. (From pages 30-31):

### A. Change in Demand (Example: Milk Market)
*   If **Demand Increases** (e.g., hot weather leads to more milk consumption):
    *   Demand curve shifts **Right** ($D_1 \rightarrow D_2$).
    *   Result: New Equilibrium has a **Higher Price** and **Higher Quantity**.
    *   *Notes:* This shortage encourages more production and investment.

### B. Change in Supply (Example: Milk Market)
*   If **Supply Decreases** (e.g., higher costs for farmers):
    *   Supply curve shifts **Left** ($S_1 \rightarrow S_2$).
    *   Result: New Equilibrium has a **Higher Price** and **Lower Quantity**.
    *   *Notes:* Shortage of 11,000 units is created, pushing prices up.

## 1.7.5 Numerical Problem (From Question Paper Page 2, Q12)
**Question:** $D = 60 - 2P$ and $S = 30 + 4P$. Find Equilibrium Price and Quantity.

**Step 1: Set Demand = Supply**
$$60 - 2P = 30 + 4P$$

**Step 2: Solve for P**
$$60 - 30 = 4P + 2P$$
$$30 = 6P$$
$$P = \mathbf{5} \text{ (Equilibrium Price)}$$

**Step 3: Solve for Q (Substitute P back into either equation)**
$$Q = 60 - 2(5) = 60 - 10$$
$$Q = \mathbf{50} \text{ (Equilibrium Quantity)}$$

**Step 4: Find Excess Supply when Price = ₹6**
*   Demand at ₹6: $60 - 2(6) = 48$
*   Supply at ₹6: $30 + 4(6) = 54$
*   **Excess Supply:** $54 - 48 = \mathbf{6} \text{ units}$.
