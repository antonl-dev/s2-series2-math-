### **NUMERICAL 1 (Question Bank - Module II, Q1)**

#### **Problem Statement:**
An activity has the following time estimates:
* Optimistic time ($t_o$) = 3 days
* Most likely time ($t_m$) = 5 days
* Pessimistic time ($t_p$) = 9 days

**Determine:**
1. Expected time ($t_e$)
2. Variance ($\sigma^2$)

---

#### **Solution:**

**1. Formula for Expected Time ($t_e$):**
$$t_e = \frac{t_o + 4t_m + t_p}{6}$$

Substituting the given values:
$$t_e = \frac{3 + 4(5) + 9}{6} = \frac{3 + 20 + 9}{6} = \frac{32}{6} = 5.33 \text{ days}$$

---

**2. Formula for Variance ($\sigma^2$):**
$$\sigma^2 = \left( \frac{t_p - t_o}{6} \right)^2$$

Substituting the given values:
$$\sigma^2 = \left( \frac{9 - 3}{6} \right)^2 = \left( \frac{6}{6} \right)^2 = 1^2 = 1 \text{ day}^2$$

---

#### **Final Answer:**
* **Expected Time ($t_e$):** **$5.33 \text{ days}$**
* **Variance ($\sigma^2$):** **$1 \text{ day}^2$**

### **NUMERICAL 2 (Question Bank - Module II, Q2)**

#### **Problem Statement:**

| Activity | Predecessor | Optimistic ($t_o$) | Most Likely ($t_m$) | Pessimistic ($t_p$) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | – | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| **B** | A | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| **C** | A | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| **D** | B, C | 4 | 6 | 8 |

**Determine:**
1. Expected time for each activity
2. Critical path
3. Expected project duration

---

#### **Solution:**

#### **Step 1: Calculate Expected Time ($t_e$) for each activity**
Using $t_e = \frac{t_o + 4t_m + t_p}{6}$:

* **Activity A:** $t_e(A) = \frac{2 + 4(4) + 6}{6} = \frac{24}{6} = 4 \text{ days}$
* **Activity B:** $t_e(B) = \frac{3 + 4(5) + 7}{6} = \frac{30}{6} = 5 \text{ days}$
* **Activity C:** $t_e(C) = \frac{2 + 4(3) + 4}{6} = \frac{18}{6} = 3 \text{ days}$
* **Activity D:** $t_e(D) = \frac{4 + 4(6) + 8}{6} = \frac{36}{6} = 6 \text{ days}$

**Summary Table:**

| Activity | Predecessor | $t_o$ | $t_m$ | $t_p$ | Expected Time ($t_e$) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | – | 2 | 4 | 6 | **4** |
| **B** | A | 3 | 5 | 7 | **5** |
| **C** | A | 2 | 3 | 4 | **3** |
| **D** | B, C | 4 | 6 | 8 | **6** |

---

#### **Step 2: Identify Network Paths and Project Duration**

```
                 [B] (te = 5)
                /   \
       [A] ────      ────> [D] (te = 6)
     (te = 4)   \   /
                 [C] (te = 3)
```

**List all possible paths through the network:**

1. **Path 1:** $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D$
   $$\text{Duration} = t_e(A) + t_e(B) + t_e(D) = 4 + 5 + 6 = 15 \text{ days}$$

2. **Path 2:** $A \rightarrow C \rightarrow D$
   $$\text{Duration} = t_e(A) + t_e(C) + t_e(D) = 4 + 3 + 6 = 13 \text{ days}$$

---

#### **Step 3: Determine Critical Path and Expected Project Duration**
The path with the longest duration is **$A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D$**.

---

#### **Final Answer:**
* **Expected Activity Durations:** $A = 4\text{ days}, B = 5\text{ days}, C = 3\text{ days}, D = 6\text{ days}$
* **Critical Path:** **$A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D$** (or **A–B–D**)
* **Expected Project Duration:** **$15 \text{ days}$**

### **NUMERICAL 3 (Internal Assessment - Question 10a)**

#### **Problem Statement:**
The table below gives activity details for a small project:

| Activity | Optimistic time ($t_o$) | Most Likely time ($t_m$) | Pessimistic time ($t_p$) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1-2** | 3 | 6 | 9 |
| **1-6** | 2 | 5 | 8 |
| **2-3** | 6 | 12 | 18 |
| **2-4** | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| **3-5** | 8 | 11 | 14 |
| **4-5** | 3 | 7 | 11 |
| **6-7** | 3 | 9 | 15 |
| **5-8** | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| **7-8** | 8 | 16 | 18 |

**Determine:**
i) Calculate time estimate ($t_e$) for each activity.  
ii) Identify the critical path.  
iii) Calculate standard deviation and variance for the critical activities.  
iv) Find the probability that the project will be completed in **35 weeks**.

---

#### **Solution:**

#### **Part (i): Calculate Expected Time ($t_e$) and Variance ($\sigma^2$) for each activity**

Formulas:
$$t_e = \frac{t_o + 4t_m + t_p}{6}, \quad \sigma^2 = \left( \frac{t_p - t_o}{6} \right)^2$$

* **Activity 1-2:** $t_e = \frac{3 + 4(6) + 9}{6} = \frac{36}{6} = 6$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{9-3}{6}\right)^2 = 1^2 = 1$
* **Activity 1-6:** $t_e = \frac{2 + 4(5) + 8}{6} = \frac{30}{6} = 5$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{8-2}{6}\right)^2 = 1^2 = 1$
* **Activity 2-3:** $t_e = \frac{6 + 4(12) + 18}{6} = \frac{72}{6} = 12$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{18-6}{6}\right)^2 = 2^2 = 4$
* **Activity 2-4:** $t_e = \frac{4 + 4(5) + 6}{6} = \frac{30}{6} = 5$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{6-4}{6}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{2}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{9} \approx 0.111$
* **Activity 3-5:** $t_e = \frac{8 + 4(11) + 14}{6} = \frac{66}{6} = 11$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{14-8}{6}\right)^2 = 1^2 = 1$
* **Activity 4-5:** $t_e = \frac{3 + 4(7) + 11}{6} = \frac{42}{6} = 7$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{11-3}{6}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{8}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{16}{9} \approx 1.778$
* **Activity 6-7:** $t_e = \frac{3 + 4(9) + 15}{6} = \frac{54}{6} = 9$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{15-3}{6}\right)^2 = 2^2 = 4$
* **Activity 5-8:** $t_e = \frac{2 + 4(4) + 6}{6} = \frac{24}{6} = 4$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{6-2}{6}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{4}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9} \approx 0.444$
* **Activity 7-8:** $t_e = \frac{8 + 4(16) + 18}{6} = \frac{90}{6} = 15$; $\quad \sigma^2 = \left(\frac{18-8}{6}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{10}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{25}{9} \approx 2.778$

---

#### **Complete Activity Computation Table:**

| Activity | $t_o$ | $t_m$ | $t_p$ | Expected Time ($t_e$) | Variance ($\sigma^2$) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1-2** | 3 | 6 | 9 | **6** | **1.000** |
| **1-6** | 2 | 5 | 8 | **5** | **1.000** |
| **2-3** | 6 | 12 | 18 | **12** | **4.000** |
| **2-4** | 4 | 5 | 6 | **5** | **0.111** |
| **3-5** | 8 | 11 | 14 | **11** | **1.000** |
| **4-5** | 3 | 7 | 11 | **7** | **1.778** |
| **6-7** | 3 | 9 | 15 | **9** | **4.000** |
| **5-8** | 2 | 4 | 6 | **4** | **0.444** |
| **7-8** | 8 | 16 | 18 | **15** | **2.778** |

---

#### **Part (ii): Identify Network Paths & Critical Path**

List all possible network paths from start event (1) to finish event (8):

1. **Path 1:** $1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 8$
   $$\text{Duration} = 6 + 12 + 11 + 4 = \mathbf{33 \text{ weeks}}$$

2. **Path 2:** $1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 4 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 8$
   $$\text{Duration} = 6 + 5 + 7 + 4 = \mathbf{22 \text{ weeks}}$$

3. **Path 3:** $1 \rightarrow 6 \rightarrow 7 \rightarrow 8$
   $$\text{Duration} = 5 + 9 + 15 = \mathbf{29 \text{ weeks}}$$

* **Critical Path:** **$1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 8$** (or **1-2-3-5-8**)
* **Expected Project Duration ($T_E$):** **$33 \text{ weeks}$**

---

#### **Part (iii): Calculate Variance and Standard Deviation for Critical Activities**

Critical activities are: **(1-2), (2-3), (3-5), and (5-8)**

* **Project Variance ($\sigma^2_{\text{proj}}$):**
  $$\sigma^2_{\text{proj}} = \sigma^2_{(1-2)} + \sigma^2_{(2-3)} + \sigma^2_{(3-5)} + \sigma^2_{(5-8)}$$
  $$\sigma^2_{\text{proj}} = 1.000 + 4.000 + 1.000 + 0.444 = \mathbf{6.444 \text{ weeks}^2} \quad \left( \text{or } \frac{58}{9} \right)$$

* **Project Standard Deviation ($\sigma_{\text{proj}}$):**
  $$\sigma_{\text{proj}} = \sqrt{\sigma^2_{\text{proj}}} = \sqrt{6.444} = \mathbf{2.5385 \text{ weeks}}$$

---

#### **Part (iv): Probability of completing the project in 35 weeks**

Given Target Completion Time ($T_d$) = **$35 \text{ weeks}$**

1. **Calculate the Standard Normal Factor ($Z$):**
   $$Z = \frac{T_d - T_E}{\sigma_{\text{proj}}} = \frac{35 - 33}{2.5385} = \frac{2}{2.5385} = \mathbf{0.7878} \approx \mathbf{0.79}$$

2. **Lookup $Z = 0.79$ in Standard Normal Distribution Table:**
   $$\text{Probability } P(Z \le 0.79) \approx 0.7852 = \mathbf{78.52\%}$$

---

#### **Final Summary Answer:**
1. **Activity time estimates:** See computation table above.
2. **Critical Path:** **$1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 8$** (Duration = **$33 \text{ weeks}$**)
3. **Variance ($\sigma^2_{\text{proj}}$):** **$6.444 \text{ weeks}^2$** | **Standard Deviation ($\sigma_{\text{proj}}$):** **$2.5385 \text{ weeks}$**
4. **Probability of completion in 35 weeks:** **$78.52\%$**
