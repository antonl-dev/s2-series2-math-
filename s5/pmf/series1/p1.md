# **BATCH 1: Module I – Short Questions (2 Marks)**

---

### **Q1. Define a project.**
**Answer:**  
A **project** is a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result. It has a well-defined beginning and end, a specific scope, allocated resources (budget and manpower), and is designed to achieve a specific objective within defined constraints of time, cost, and quality.

---

### **Q2. List any four characteristics of a project.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Uniqueness:** Every project produces a distinct deliverable or outcome that differs from routine operations.
2. **Temporary Duration:** It has a definitive start and end date.
3. **Defined Scope and Objective:** Specific deliverables and criteria must be met to achieve success.
4. **Interdisciplinary Team / Resource Constraints:** It requires cross-functional expertise and operates under limited budget, human resources, and time.

---

### **Q3. State any two importance of project management.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Efficient Resource Utilization:** Ensures optimal allocation and utilization of time, money, and manpower, minimizing waste.
2. **Risk Mitigation and Quality Control:** Helps identify potential risks early and maintains quality standards to deliver outcomes on schedule and within budget.

---

### **Q4. Define project life cycle.**
**Answer:**  
A **project life cycle** is the high-level sequence of phases that a project passes through from its inception to its completion. It provides a structured framework for managing project work, typically comprising initiation, planning, execution, monitoring & controlling, and closure.

---

### **Q5. List the phases of the project life cycle.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Initiation Phase:** Defining project scope, feasibility, and charter approval.
2. **Planning Phase:** Developing schedules, work breakdown structures (WBS), resource plans, and budgets.
3. **Execution Phase:** Implementing the project plan and executing deliverables.
4. **Monitoring and Controlling Phase:** Tracking progress, controlling variations, and managing changes.
5. **Closure Phase:** Handing over deliverables, releasing resources, and conducting post-project evaluation.

---

### **Q6. State two differences between project management and general management.**
**Answer:**  

| Feature | Project Management | General Management |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature of Work** | Temporary, unique, and non-repetitive activities. | Continuous, ongoing, and repetitive operations. |
| **Organizational Structure** | Temporary, cross-functional teams dissolved after completion. | Permanent hierarchical functional departments (e.g., HR, Finance). |

---

### **Q7. Classify projects based on ownership with suitable examples.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Public Sector Projects:** Financed, owned, and operated by the government aimed at public welfare.  
   *Example:* Construction of a national highway, Metro Rail network.
2. **Private Sector Projects:** Owned and funded by private individuals or business enterprises for profit motive.  
   *Example:* Setting up a commercial shopping mall, software development by a private IT firm.
3. **Joint Sector Projects:** Jointly owned and managed by government agencies and private entrepreneurs.  
   *Example:* Public-Private Partnership (PPP) power generation projects.

---

### **Q8. What is project feasibility analysis? State its objectives.**
**Answer:**  
**Project Feasibility Analysis** is a comprehensive evaluation process carried out before project implementation to assess whether a proposed project is technically doable, financially viable, market-demanded, and operationally executable.  
**Objectives:**
* To evaluate the technical, financial, and operational viability of a project idea.
* To prevent investment failures by identifying potential risks and constraints beforehand.

---

### **Q9. What is project identification? Mention its significance.**
**Answer:**  
**Project Identification** is the preliminary stage in project management where potential business ideas or opportunities are generated, screened, and selected based on market demand, societal needs, and resource availability.  
**Significance:**  
* Ensures investment is made in high-priority and profitable ventures aligned with organizational goals.
* Prevents resource wastage on non-viable project ideas right at the initial stage.

---

### **Q10. List any four challenges encountered during project execution.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Scope Creep:** Uncontrolled expansion of project scope without adjustments to time and budget.
2. **Resource Constraints:** Inadequate availability or misallocation of skilled manpower, equipment, or funds.
3. **Schedule Delays:** Unexpected bottlenecks leading to timeline overruns.
4. **Poor Communication:** Misalignment between key stakeholders, team members, and client expectations.

---

### **Q11. Mention two key responsibilities of a project manager.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Project Planning and Scheduling:** Defining tasks, allocating resources, establishing realistic deadlines, and setting performance metrics.
2. **Monitoring and Risk Management:** Tracking project progress against baselines, managing constraints, and mitigating risks to ensure on-time, within-budget completion.

---

### **Q12. Explain any two types of feasibility analysis in projects.**
**Answer:**  
1. **Technical Feasibility:** Assesses whether the required technology, equipment, technical skills, and operational infrastructure are available to execute the project successfully.
2. **Financial Feasibility:** Evaluates total financial outlay, expected revenue, profitability, capital requirements, cash flow forecasts, and return on investment (ROI).

---

### **Q13. An IT firm wants to improve efficiency in project execution. Discuss the role of project management in achieving this.**
**Answer:**  
Project management provides structured frameworks (like Agile, Waterfall, WBS, and Gantt charts) that establish clear task ownership, streamline resource allocation, minimize rework, and reduce project bottlenecks. By continuously monitoring performance indicators and tracking bugs/deliverables, it reduces schedule slippage and ensures timely client delivery.
