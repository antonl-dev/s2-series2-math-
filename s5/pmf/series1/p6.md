### **NUMERICAL 1 (Question Bank - Module II, Q3)**

#### **Problem Statement:**
A project consists of the following activities:

| Activity | Duration (days) |
| :---: | :---: |
| **1-2** | 4 |
| **1-3** | 1 |
| **2-4** | 1 |
| **3-4** | 1 |
| **3-5** | 6 |

**Determine:**
1. Draw the network diagram.
2. Calculate ES, EF, LS, and LF for each activity.
3. Determine the Total Float ($TF$) for each activity.
4. Identify the Critical Path and project duration.

---

#### **Solution:**

#### **1. Network Diagram:**

```
               [2] ──(4)──> [2] ──(1)──> [4]
             /                          ^
          (4)                          (1)
          /                              \
      (1) ───(1)───> [3] ──(1)───────────┘
                       │
                      (6)
                       │
                       ▼
                      [5]
```

---

#### **2. Event Times (Forward & Backward Pass):**

* **Forward Pass (Calculate Early Event Times $E_i$):**
  * $E_1 = 0$
  * $E_2 = E_1 + 4 = 4$
  * $E_3 = E_1 + 1 = 1$
  * $E_4 = \max(E_2 + 1, E_3 + 1) = \max(4 + 1, 1 + 1) = \max(5, 2) = 5$
  * $E_5 = E_3 + 6 = 1 + 6 = 7$

* **Project Duration ($T_P$):** $\max(E_4, E_5) = \max(5, 7) = \mathbf{7 \text{ days}}$

* **Backward Pass (Calculate Late Event Times $L_i$):**
  * $L_5 = 7, \quad L_4 = 7$
  * $L_3 = \min(L_4 - 1, L_5 - 6) = \min(7 - 1, 7 - 6) = \min(6, 1) = 1$
  * $L_2 = L_4 - 1 = 7 - 1 = 6$
  * $L_1 = \min(L_2 - 4, L_3 - 1) = \min(6 - 4, 1 - 1) = \min(2, 0) = 0$

---

#### **3. Activity Schedule Table (ES, EF, LS, LF, Total Float):**

Formulas:
* $\text{Early Start } (ES) = E_i$
* $\text{Early Finish } (EF) = ES + t_{ij}$
* $\text{Late Finish } (LF) = L_j$
* $\text{Late Start } (LS) = LF - t_{ij}$
* $\text{Total Float } (TF) = LS - ES = LF - EF$

| Activity ($i-j$) | Duration ($t_{ij}$) | Early Start ($ES$) | Early Finish ($EF$) | Late Start ($LS$) | Late Finish ($LF$) | Total Float ($TF$) | Critical? |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1-2** | 4 | 0 | 4 | 2 | 6 | **2** | No |
| **1-3** | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | **0** | **YES** |
| **2-4** | 1 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | **2** | No |
| **3-4** | 1 | 1 | 2 | 6 | 7 | **5** | No |
| **3-5** | 6 | 1 | 7 | 1 | 7 | **0** | **YES** |

---

#### **4. Critical Path:**
Activities with **$TF = 0$** are critical activities: **1-3** and **3-5**.

* **Critical Path:** **$1 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 5$** (or **1-3-5**)
* **Project Duration:** **$7 \text{ days}$**

### **NUMERICAL 2 (Internal Assessment - Question 9a)**

#### **Problem Statement:**
A construction project consists of the following activities:

| Activity | Duration (days) | Predecessors |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | 4 | – |
| **B** | 6 | A |
| **C** | 5 | A |
| **D** | 3 | B |
| **E** | 7 | C |
| **F** | 4 | D, E |

**Determine:**
i) Identify the critical path and project duration.  
ii) Perform forward and backward passes to find ES, EF, LS, and LF.  
iii) Find total float ($TF$) and free float ($FF$) for each activity.

---

#### **Solution:**

#### **Step 1: Network Path Duration Analysis**

List all paths through the project network:
1. **Path 1:** $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D \rightarrow F$
   $$\text{Duration} = 4 + 6 + 3 + 4 = 17 \text{ days}$$

2. **Path 2:** $A \rightarrow C \rightarrow E \rightarrow F$
   $$\text{Duration} = 4 + 5 + 7 + 4 = \mathbf{20 \text{ days}}$$

* **Critical Path:** **$A \rightarrow C \rightarrow E \rightarrow F$**
* **Project Duration:** **$20 \text{ days}$**

---

#### **Step 2: Activity Schedule & Float Calculation Table**

Formulas:
* $ES = \max(EF \text{ of immediate predecessors})$
* $EF = ES + \text{Duration}$
* $LF = \min(LS \text{ of immediate successors})$
* $LS = LF - \text{Duration}$
* $\text{Total Float } (TF) = LS - ES = LF - EF$
* $\text{Free Float } (FF) = ES(\text{immediate successor}) - EF$

| Activity | Duration | $ES$ | $EF$ | $LS$ | $LF$ | Total Float ($TF$) | Free Float ($FF$) | Critical? |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | 4 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 4 | **0** | **0** | **YES** |
| **B** | 6 | 4 | 10 | 7 | 13 | **3** | **0** | No |
| **C** | 5 | 4 | 9 | 4 | 9 | **0** | **0** | **YES** |
| **D** | 3 | 10 | 13 | 13 | 16 | **3** | **3** | No |
| **E** | 7 | 9 | 16 | 9 | 16 | **0** | **0** | **YES** |
| **F** | 4 | 16 | 20 | 16 | 20 | **0** | **0** | **YES** |

---

#### **Detailed Free Float Calculations:**
* **$FF(A)$** $= \min(ES_B, ES_C) - EF_A = \min(4, 4) - 4 = 0$
* **$FF(B)$** $= ES_D - EF_B = 10 - 10 = 0$
* **$FF(C)$** $= ES_E - EF_C = 9 - 9 = 0$
* **$FF(D)$** $= ES_F - EF_D = 16 - 13 = \mathbf{3}$
* **$FF(E)$** $= ES_F - EF_E = 16 - 16 = 0$
* **$FF(F)$** $= \text{Project Finish} - EF_F = 20 - 20 = 0$

---

#### **Final Answer:**
1. **Critical Path:** **$A \rightarrow C \rightarrow E \rightarrow F$** (or **A-C-E-F**)
2. **Project Duration:** **$20 \text{ days}$**
3. **Activity Schedule Table:** See table above.

### **NUMERICAL 3 (Internal Assessment - Question 12b)**

#### **Problem Statement:**
Consider the data of a project shown below. If the **indirect cost per week is Rs. 200**, find the **optimal crashed project completion time** and the corresponding **minimum total project cost**.

| Activity | Normal Time (wks) | Normal Cost (Rs.) | Crash Time (wks) | Crash Cost (Rs.) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1-2** | 7 | 700 | 4 | 850 |
| **1-3** | 5 | 500 | 3 | 700 |
| **1-4** | 8 | 600 | 5 | 1200 |
| **2-5** | 9 | 800 | 7 | 1250 |
| **3-5** | 5 | 700 | 3 | 1000 |
| **3-6** | 6 | 1100 | 5 | 1300 |
| **4-6** | 7 | 1200 | 5 | 1450 |
| **5-7** | 2 | 400 | 1 | 500 |
| **6-7** | 3 | 500 | 2 | 850 |

---

#### **Solution:**

#### **Step 1: Calculate Cost Slopes and Maximum Crashable Limits**

$$\text{Cost Slope} = \frac{\text{Crash Cost } (CC) - \text{Normal Cost } (NC)}{\text{Normal Time } (NT) - \text{Crash Time } (CT)}$$

| Activity | $NT$ | $NC$ | $CT$ | $CC$ | Max Crash ($NT - CT$) | Cost Slope (Rs./week) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1-2** | 7 | 700 | 4 | 850 | 3 | $(850-700)/3 = \mathbf{50}$ |
| **1-3** | 5 | 500 | 3 | 700 | 2 | $(700-500)/2 = \mathbf{100}$ |
| **1-4** | 8 | 600 | 5 | 1200 | 3 | $(1200-600)/3 = \mathbf{200}$ |
| **2-5** | 9 | 800 | 7 | 1250 | 2 | $(1250-800)/2 = \mathbf{225}$ |
| **3-5** | 5 | 700 | 3 | 1000 | 2 | $(1000-700)/2 = \mathbf{150}$ |
| **3-6** | 6 | 1100 | 5 | 1300 | 1 | $(1300-1100)/1 = \mathbf{200}$ |
| **4-6** | 7 | 1200 | 5 | 1450 | 2 | $(1450-1200)/2 = \mathbf{125}$ |
| **5-7** | 2 | 400 | 1 | 500 | 1 | $(500-400)/1 = \mathbf{100}$ |
| **6-7** | 3 | 500 | 2 | 850 | 1 | $(850-500)/1 = \mathbf{350}$ |

* **Total Normal Direct Cost ($\sum NC$):**
  $$700 + 500 + 600 + 800 + 700 + 1100 + 1200 + 400 + 500 = \mathbf{\text{Rs. } 6500}$$

---

#### **Step 2: Identify Network Paths (Normal Duration)**

List all paths through the network from node 1 to node 7:
* **Path I:** $1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 7 = 7 + 9 + 2 = \mathbf{18 \text{ weeks}}$
* **Path II:** $1 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 7 = 5 + 5 + 2 = \mathbf{12 \text{ weeks}}$
* **Path III:** $1 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 6 \rightarrow 7 = 5 + 6 + 3 = \mathbf{14 \text{ weeks}}$
* **Path IV:** $1 \rightarrow 4 \rightarrow 6 \rightarrow 7 = 8 + 7 + 3 = \mathbf{18 \text{ weeks}}$

**Initial Normal State:**
* **Critical Paths:** **Path I** ($1-2-5-7$) and **Path IV** ($1-4-6-7$), both at **18 weeks**.
* **Direct Cost:** Rs. 6500
* **Indirect Cost:** $18 \times 200 = \text{Rs. } 3600$
* **Total Project Cost:** $6500 + 3600 = \mathbf{\text{Rs. } 10,100}$

---

#### **Step 3: Systematic Crashing Iterations**

To compress the project, we must simultaneously reduce activities on **ALL active critical paths** (Path I and Path IV) where the combined cost slope is less than the indirect savings of **Rs. 200/week**.

---

#### **Iteration 1:**
* **Critical Paths:** Path I ($1-2-5-7$) and Path IV ($1-4-6-7$) [Duration = 18 weeks]
* **Options to crash:**
  * Crash **(1-2)** on Path I [Slope = 50] **AND** crash **(4-6)** on Path IV [Slope = 125].
  * Combined Cost Slope = $50 + 125 = \mathbf{\text{Rs. } 175/\text{week}}$.
  * Since $175 < 200$ (Indirect savings), crashing is **cost-effective**.
* **Action:** Crash Activity (1-2) by 2 weeks (avail: 3) and Activity (4-6) by 2 weeks (avail: 2).
* **New Durations:**
  * Path I: $5 + 9 + 2 = 16 \text{ weeks}$
  * Path IV: $8 + 5 + 3 = 16 \text{ weeks}$
* **New Costs for 16 weeks:**
  * Direct Cost Increase = $2 \times 175 = \text{Rs. } 350$
  * New Direct Cost = $6500 + 350 = \text{Rs. } 6850$
  * Indirect Cost = $16 \times 200 = \text{Rs. } 3200$
  * **Total Cost:** $6850 + 3200 = \mathbf{\text{Rs. } 10,050}$ *(Savings of Rs. 50)*

---

#### **Iteration 2:**
* **Critical Paths:** Path I ($1-2-5-7$) and Path IV ($1-4-6-7$) [Duration = 16 weeks]
* **Remaining available crashes:**
  * Path I: (1-2) has 1 week remaining [Slope = 50].
  * Path IV: (1-4) has 3 weeks [Slope = 200]; (4-6) is fully crashed.
* **Options to crash:**
  * Crash **(1-2)** [Slope = 50] on Path I **AND** crash **(1-4)** [Slope = 200] on Path IV.
  * Combined Cost Slope = $50 + 200 = \mathbf{\text{Rs. } 250/\text{week}}$.
  * Since $250 > 200$ (Indirect savings), crashing further will **increase total project cost**.

* **Alternative Check:** Can we crash a common end node?
  * Crash **(5-7)** on Path I/II [Slope = 100] **AND** crash **(1-4)** [Slope = 200] on Path IV $\implies 100 + 200 = 300 > 200$. (Not economical).

---

#### **Cost Optimization Summary Table:**

| Stage / Duration | Critical Paths | Direct Cost (Rs.) | Indirect Cost (Rs. 200/wk) | Total Project Cost (Rs.) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Normal (18 weeks)** | Path I, Path IV | 6,500 | 3,600 | **10,100** |
| **Crashed to 16 weeks** | Path I, Path IV | 6,850 | 3,200 | **10,050 (OPTIMAL)** |
| **Crashed to 15 weeks** | Path I, Path IV | 7,100 | 3,000 | **10,100** |

---

#### **Final Answer:**
* **Optimal Project Completion Duration:** **$16 \text{ weeks}$**
* **Minimum Total Project Cost:** **$\text{Rs. } 10,050$**
* **Activities Crashed:** Activity **(1-2)** by **2 weeks** and Activity **(4-6)** by **2 weeks**.
